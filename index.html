Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Consnow ("we", "our", or "the app") is a social status-sharing application developed by CodeHub Labs. This privacy policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Consnow, we collect the following types of data:

Account Information

  • Email address and display name (provided during registration)
  • Unique connection code (generated by the app for connecting with friends)

Health & Fitness Data

  • Daily step count — collected using your device's built-in step counter sensor via the Android Activity Recognition API

Device & Activity Data

  • Phone lock/unlock status — whether your phone screen is currently locked or unlocked
  • Last active time — the last time your phone was unlocked

Connection Data

  • Your friends list — the users you have mutually connected with using the in-app connection code

Technical Data

  • Authentication tokens (JWT) stored locally on your device for session management

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data solely to provide the core functionality of Consnow:

  • To display your step count and activity status to your connected friends
  • To display your connected friends' step count and activity status to you
  • To authenticate your account and maintain your session
  • To sync your data between your device and our servers so your friends can see your latest status

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, analytics, or any purpose other than delivering the app's core features.

3. Data Sharing

Your activity data (step count, lock/unlock status, and last active time) is shared only with users you have mutually connected with inside the app. Both users must consent to the connection by exchanging a unique code.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Your data is stored on secure servers hosted on Railway (cloud infrastructure provider).
  • All communication between the app and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Authentication tokens are stored locally on your device using Android's encrypted DataStore.
  • Step data is also cached locally on your device using an encrypted Room database for offline access.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all associated data (step records, connection data, and account information) will be permanently removed from our servers within 30 days.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data — all your data is visible within the app
  • Delete your account and all associated data by contacting us
  • Disconnect from any friend at any time, which immediately stops sharing your status with them
  • Revoke permissions — you can revoke the Activity Recognition permission at any time through your device settings, which will stop step counting

7. Permissions We Request

  • Activity Recognition — to count your steps using the device sensor
  • Foreground Service (Health) — to keep counting steps in the background
  • Notifications — to show a persistent notification while counting steps (required by Android)
  • Internet — to sync your data with our servers and your connected friends

8. Children's Privacy

Consnow is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, we will promptly delete that data.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
